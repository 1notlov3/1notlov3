How it listens

One breath.

A real morning, taken down as it was spoken. Order is time — nothing else to learn.

  1. 0:00

    “Morning. I had a latte and a banana on the way in.”

  2. 0:03

    kcalm Logged — latte with whole milk, plus a banana. About 340 calories, 11 grams of protein.

  3. 0:07

    “Actually it was oat milk. And the banana was pretty small.”

  4. 0:09

    kcalm Fixed. Oat-milk latte, small banana — 290 calories now. Your coffee order remains impeccable.

  5. 0:12

    “Where does that put me today?”

  6. 0:14

    kcalm 290 so far, 1,710 to go. Protein’s at 8 grams — plenty of day left to fix that.

Fourteen seconds. No forms. No search. No barcode gymnastics.
